How AgroLink works

A straightforward first step toward more transparent agricultural trade.

01

Share supply or demand

Farmers list produce and expected availability. Buyers describe crop, quantity, quality and delivery location.

02

Discover potential matches

Search and filters help people find relevant listings and requests by crop, volume and state.

03

Agree and coordinate

Parties verify details, agree on price and delivery terms, and arrange fulfilment directly. This prototype does not handle payments.
